Services

What we survey

Each report separates what was observed from what caused it, and sets out proportionate remedial works in the right sequence, so landlords, solicitors, homeowners and buyers can act on it with confidence.

01

Damp and mould surveys

Diagnosis of condensation, penetrating and rising damp, ventilation shortfalls and thermal bridging, with moisture readings, HHSRS assessment and targeted remedial recommendations.

02

Housing disrepair and CPR Part 35

Independent disrepair inspections and expert reports for court proceedings, with Scott Schedules setting out defects and remedial works.

03

Structural crack assessment

Crack mapping, categorisation and assessment of likely cause, with masonry repair designs including helical crack-stitching and lintel replacement where appropriate.

04

Condition surveys

Elemental and stock condition surveys of housing and corporate buildings, recording condition, remaining life and planned maintenance priorities.

05

Roof and defect diagnosis

Investigation of roof defects, water ingress and weathering failures, identifying root causes rather than symptoms, with repair options and sequencing.

Homebuyer surveys

Pre-purchase inspections of houses and flats, reporting on condition, defects, damp and repairs needed, so you know what you are buying before you commit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a damp and mould survey involve?

We inspect the whole property, not just the affected rooms, so that the building is understood as a whole. This includes the building fabric, moisture and humidity readings, ventilation, heating and insulation, and external elements such as roofs, gutters and pointing. The report identifies the source of the moisture and sets out the remedial works needed to deal with the cause, not only the mould.

How do you tell condensation from rising or penetrating damp?

Each has a different pattern. Condensation forms on cold surfaces where moist air is not removed, and is often worst in corners, behind furniture and around windows. Penetrating damp is linked to a defect such as a leaking gutter, roof or failed pointing. Rising damp is confined to the base of ground floor walls. We use readings, the pattern of staining and the condition of the building to identify which is present, as more than one cause can occur together.

What is a CPR Part 35 report?

It is an expert report prepared for court proceedings under Part 35 of the Civil Procedure Rules. The expert's duty is to the court, not to the party paying for the report, so the evidence must be independent and objective. In housing disrepair cases the report is usually accompanied by a Scott Schedule listing each defect, its cause and the remedial works.

What does Awaab's Law mean for landlords?

Awaab's Law requires social landlords in England to investigate and repair damp and mould hazards within set legal timescales. A survey that identifies the cause and sets out the remedial works helps landlords respond within those timescales and keep a clear record of the action taken.

Are cracks in my walls serious?

Most cracks are minor and caused by shrinkage or thermal movement, but some indicate structural movement such as settlement, subsidence or a failed lintel. We record the width, pattern and location of each crack, assess the likely cause and advise whether repair, monitoring or further investigation is needed.

Do I need a homebuyer survey?

A mortgage valuation is carried out for the lender and does not report on the condition of the property. A homebuyer survey tells you about defects, damp and repairs needed before you commit, so you can budget for them or negotiate on price.

How quickly will I receive my report?

Reports are usually issued within 3 to 5 working days of inspection. Urgent and court deadlines can be accommodated, so please tell us about any deadline when you get in touch.
